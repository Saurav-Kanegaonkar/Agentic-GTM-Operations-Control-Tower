Operating thesis

what the model is saying

The first deployment should be a workflow with strong confidence, clean source ownership, and a measurable revenue motion. Higher upside workflows remain valuable, but the control tower separates attractive pilots from workflows that are ready for a recurring operating cadence.

That distinction matters because the role is not to recommend more automation. It is to ship automation that leaders can trust, measure, and keep using.
